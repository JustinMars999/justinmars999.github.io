01 / ABOUT

Making measurements
matter.

My research brings together robotic perception, environmental sensing and adaptive measurement for infrastructure inspection.

I work with ROS 2, Unitree robots, LiDAR and camera data, alongside environmental-sensing experiments and gas-dispersion simulations. I’m interested in how sensor response, sampling height and robot motion affect the information we collect, and how those measurements can support gas-source localisation.

Before my PhD, I studied mechatronics at the University of Glasgow and Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman (UTAR). My projects have grown from a Bluetooth robot car built at age 16 to connected hydroponics and quadruped robotic sensing.
